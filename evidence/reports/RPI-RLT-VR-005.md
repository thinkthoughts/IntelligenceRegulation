## 1. Summary

1. **The Lean library is reported to build completely on a 15 GiB machine**, and the central theorem is reported
   to depend on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` only. These are the fork owner's results from a
   separate VPS. Their logs are still to be placed in the repository, so this report records them as *reported*
   and gives the exact transfer steps (section 2).
2. **The theorem `stgAlg_productionReadyL_epP_exec_real` is a conditional theorem about a parametric verifier.**
   Its statement has 156 explicit binders. Read from the source text, 85 binders of the statement are named
   hypotheses, two more come from the file's section variables, and one binder, `O`, is a bundle of 12 further
   obligations. This review classifies 103 items (section 4).
3. **26 of the 103 are open cryptographic or protocol obligations.** They fall into eight groups: stage soundness
   and stage facts on the run's transcript, the wiring of the block theorem into the run and the block facts,
   the input endpoint's extractor, the remaining count conditions, completeness, and zero knowledge of the run
   (section 4.2).
4. **18 are already proved in the repository, on other lines of work.** The repository's own finding L56-2 says
   it: three parallel restated top theorems exist, each with some discharges, and no single theorem carries all
   of them. The theorem audited here still lists, as hypotheses, facts that `eb_productionReadyL_epP_codeT` and
   `cb_productionReadyL_epP_codeF` have discharged.
5. **Tooling is in place and tested**: a Lean command that writes the inventory as JSON, a checker that compares a
   classification with it, and nine positive and negative tests (section 5). The Lean command needs the compiled
   top module, which exists on the VPS; one command there completes the inventory.

A theorem with a checked proof term and a guarantee about the running application are different things. The
first is what Lean confirms: *if* every hypothesis holds, the conclusion follows. The second needs every
hypothesis established for one concrete instance: the Shieldstral T128 verifier written as Lean values in place
of the theorem's parameters. The repository's gap audits say the same (GA-4, GB-3); this report adds a named,
checkable list of what stands between the two.
